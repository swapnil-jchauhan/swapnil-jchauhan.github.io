Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Jarvis Assistant ("the app") is a personal assistant that runs only on its developer's own computer and is used only by its developer.

Google data the app can access

With the owner's approval, the app can read and change the owner's Google Calendar events, read, draft and send the owner's Gmail messages, read, download and upload the owner's Google Drive files, and read the owner's YouTube likes, subscriptions and playlists.

How the data is used

Data is used only to carry out the owner's own spoken requests (for example "add a meeting tomorrow at five" or "what is in my inbox"). Parts of an email or file may be sent to an AI language model service (Groq) to summarise or draft text at the owner's request. Data is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with any other person or company.

Storage

The app keeps a Google sign-in token on the owner's own computer. The developer runs no server and receives no data. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or by deleting the token file.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
